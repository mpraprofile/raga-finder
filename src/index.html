    Swara names & numbering
    NameCode

    Some schools number Gandhara/Nishada variants by pitch order (Shuddha=1, being lowest); others by frequency of use instead, since Shuddha is rare/vivadi - pushing it to "3" and cycling Sadharana/Kaisiki and Antara/Kakali down to "1" and "2". Pick your convention using the Shuddha Gandhara / Shuddha Nishada rows above - the other rows in each family update to match. This choice changes every place a swara is shown across the app: the chart below, the input widgets, and every raga's own scale in the finder's results - it's just a relabeling of the same scale, not a change to what the scale is.

    The 72 melakartas

    Every parent scale, arranged so its own recipe is readable off the chart: the sector fixes Rishabha and Gandhara, the ring fixes Dhaivata and Nishada, and which side of the line you are on fixes Madhyama. Sa, Pa and the upper Sa never vary, which is why the middle is empty.
