    NameCode

    Some schools number Gandhara/Nishada variants by pitch order (Shuddha=1, being lowest); others by frequency of use instead, since Shuddha is rare/vivadi - pushing it to "3" and cycling Sadharana/Kaisiki and Antara/Kakali down to "1" and "2". Pick your convention using the Shuddha Gandhara / Shuddha Nishada rows above - the other rows in each family update to match. This choice changes every place a swara is shown across the app, including each raga's own result further down - it's just a relabeling of the same scale, not a change to what the scale is.
